🚗 THE DRIVE

This route skips Washington, DC and I-95 entirely by going west through the mountains. Most drivers report roughly 11 hours total from the Triangle, about the same as the route through DC and without the Beltway traffic. Always check live traffic and weather before you go.

FRI20NOV
Day 1: Cary ➜ Charleston, WV (about 6 hrs)
  • I-40 West toward Greensboro, then US-52 North / I-74 to Mount Airy, NC.
  • I-77 North through Virginia (Fancy Gap, Wytheville, Bluefield) and into West Virginia.
  • Stop overnight near Charleston, WV. Book the hotel ahead.
OPEN DAY 1 MAP
SAT21NOV
Day 2: Charleston ➜ Buffalo (about 5.5 hrs)
  • I-79 North through West Virginia and past Pittsburgh to Erie, PA.
  • I-90 East along Lake Erie into Buffalo, then to the hotel at 4600 Genesee St.
  • Check-in starts at 3:00 PM, then the Bills Store and Duff’s.
OPEN DAY 2 MAP
FRI27NOV
Going home, day 1: Buffalo ➜ Charleston, WV
  • Check out by 11:00 AM. Reverse the route: I-90 West to Erie, then I-79 South.
OPEN MAP
SAT28NOV
Going home, day 2: Charleston ➜ Cary
  • I-77 South to Mount Airy, then US-52 / I-74 and I-40 East back to Cary.
OPEN MAP

Late November: the mountain stretches on I-77 (Fancy Gap) and I-79 can have fog or snow, so keep the tires, scarf and snacks handy.
